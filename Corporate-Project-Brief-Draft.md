1. Company and Project Overview GRAPHITE INDUSTRIES LIMITED is developing a graphite-focused mineral-processing and value-addition project concept in Mpwapwa District, Dodoma Region, Tanzania. The proposed initiative is intended to assess opportunities across graphite ore supply, beneficiation and potential downstream product development, subject to technical studies, regulatory approvals, commercial evaluation and investment decisions.

The project is at a development and planning stage. This brief is intended to support preliminary discussions and does not represent an operating mine or commissioned processing facility.

2. Strategic Direction The company’s stated direction is to evaluate an integrated graphite value chain rather than assume a single product or market in advance. The scope and sequence of development will depend on geological and mineralogical evidence, metallurgical test work, market specifications, infrastructure assessments, permitting requirements and funding.

Potential areas for evaluation include:

- Geological and mineralogical characterisation of graphite-bearing material.
- Metallurgical test work and beneficiation flowsheet development.
- Evaluation of concentrate quality, recovery and potential product specifications.
- Assessment of downstream value-addition opportunities where technically and commercially justified.
- Engagement with prospective buyers, technology providers, logistics operators and strategic investors.

No specific graphite product grades, recoveries, reserves, sales volumes, prices or customer commitments are asserted in this draft.

3. Project Development and Technical Workstreams The following workstreams are proposed for evaluation. Their inclusion is not a claim that the work has been completed or contracted.

Geology and feedstock verification Confirm the relevant mineral rights and lawful access to the deposit or feedstock source; compile available geological information; undertake representative sampling; and establish the appropriate resource or exploration work programme through qualified professionals.

Mineralogy and metallurgical testing Characterise representative samples and assess liberation, beneficiation response, potential recovery, product quality and process variability. Test results should inform the process design basis and any subsequent equipment selection.

Process and infrastructure planning Develop a concept-level flowsheet and evaluate water supply, grid electricity, access roads, transport options, tailings and waste management, site drainage, worker safety, dust control and environmental protection. Site measurements and utility capacities must be established through appropriate investigations.

Permitting, land and safeguards Verify land rights and site boundaries; identify applicable mining, environmental, planning, water, occupational health and safety, construction and other statutory requirements; and determine the required approvals with the relevant Tanzanian authorities.

Commercial and investment assessment Engage prospective technology providers, industrial customers, traders, logistics partners and investors to test product requirements, delivery conditions, project scope and possible cooperation structures. No financing or offtake commitment is represented as secured.

4. Infrastructure, Logistics and Market Access Mpwapwa District is within Tanzania’s central region of the country. Road and rail options, including the wider central transport corridor and relevant railway networks, may be assessed as part of a logistics study. Actual route availability, terminal access, costs, transit times and suitability for graphite products remain to be confirmed.

Infrastructure planning should consider dependable electricity, water sourcing and recycling, communications, workforce access, safe product storage, packaging and transport. No utility connection, capacity, tariff or logistics agreement is claimed in this brief.

5. Sustainability and Responsible Development The project should incorporate environmental and social considerations from the earliest planning stage. Topics for assessment include water stewardship, dust and noise control, energy efficiency, waste and tailings management, rehabilitation planning, biodiversity, community engagement, occupational health and safety, local employment, skills development and gender inclusion.

Specific targets, baseline data and commitments should be published only after the company has defined them, assessed feasibility and obtained any required approvals.

6. Partnership Opportunities GRAPHITE INDUSTRIES LIMITED welcomes preliminary dialogue with suitable organisations that can contribute to project evaluation and development. Potential engagement areas include:

- Geological services, sampling and laboratory analysis.
- Mineralogical characterisation, metallurgical testing and flowsheet development.
- Process equipment and engineering services.
- Infrastructure, utilities, water and environmental services.
- Industrial customers and prospective graphite-product buyers.
- Logistics, export and supply-chain partners.
- Strategic investors and project-development partners.

Any cooperation would be subject to due diligence, scope definition, mutually agreed terms and applicable laws and approvals.
