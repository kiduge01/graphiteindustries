WHO WE ARE

A Tanzanian company.
An industrial purpose.

Graphite Industries Limited is developing a graphite-focused processing and value-addition business in Tanzania. Our proposed project in Mpwapwa, Dodoma, will assess how suitable feedstock could become materials for industrial markets.

Technical studies, customer requirements and responsible partnerships will guide development, with opportunities for skills and local participation.

OUR PURPOSE

Our vision. Our mission.

Our vision

To become a recognised Tanzanian graphite processing facility and value-addition enterprise connecting mineral resources with regional and global industrial markets.

Our mission

To develop competitive graphite processing capabilities through responsible resource development, appropriate technologies, strategic partnerships, quality-focused operations and continuous improvement.
