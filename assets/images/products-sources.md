# Products & Applications imagery

Selected online images are stored locally in `public/assets/products/`; the live page does not hotlink third-party image hosts. No visible image captions or text overlays are added. Descriptive alternative text is retained. Material and application photographs are illustrative, not company product samples or evidence of industrial qualification.

The visitor-accessible attribution page is `public/image-credits.html`, linked from the Products footer in both the PHP response and JavaScript render. That page links authors, original image pages, applicable licences and the modified expandable-graphite copy. The USGS image is public domain; the expandable-graphite image is CC BY-SA 4.0 and remains under that licence; application photographs are under the Pexels licence.
